HOW TO WIRE A TEXTURE IN index.html
=====================================================================

1. Uncomment the <img> tag in <a-assets>:
       <img id="tex-balloon" src="assets/textures/balloon.png">

2. On the geometry element, replace the color="" attribute with:
       material="src: #tex-balloon; roughness: 0.6; metalness: 0.05"

3. For the sky, replace color="" on <a-sky>:
       <a-sky src="#tex-sky-l1"></a-sky>
   (and set it back in JS with setAttribute('src', '#tex-sky-l2')
    instead of setAttribute('color', ...) when switching levels)

4. For the ground planes, add the repeat parameter:
       material="src: #tex-ground-l1; repeat: 40 80; roughness: 1.0"
